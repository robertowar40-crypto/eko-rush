The game

Lagos traffic, but you're the fastest thing on the road.

Three lanes, endless hustle. Every run takes you past real Lagos landmarks, rebuilt in 3D and lit up at night.

Danfo, keke and BRT

Yellow danfos, keke napep and BRT buses fill the road. Jump over them, roll under them, or ride the ramps onto their roofs.

Real landmarks

National Theatre, NECOM House, TBS, Civic Centre, Eko Atlantic, plus both bridges over the lagoon.

Collect naira, grab power-ups

Grab ₦ coins and pick up magnets, super sneakers and 2X multipliers to push your high score.

Day and night

Switch themes and the city changes with it: at night the street lamps glow, windows light up and the Link Bridge shines purple.

Jetpack boosters

Grab a jetpack and fly above the danfos for eight seconds, collecting a trail of naira in the sky.

Race a friend

Create a match, send the link or code, and race on the exact same course. Higher score wins.

How to play

Swipe or use the arrow keys.

Works on desktop, tablet and phone. Bump into the side of a vehicle twice and LASTMA catches you.

S or swipe down. Gets you under the ÈKÓ signs.
